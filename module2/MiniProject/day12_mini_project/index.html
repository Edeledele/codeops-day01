The coffee ceremony

Ethiopia is the birthplace of coffee, and at Habesha Eatery the ceremony is part of the meal, not an afterthought. Beans are roasted table-side, ground by hand, and brewed in a clay jebena over hot coals.

Ask your server about pairing the ceremony with your reservation, especially for groups of four or more.
