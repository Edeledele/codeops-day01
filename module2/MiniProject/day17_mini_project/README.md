## Loyalty Points Module

The loyalty module is designed for a **TeleBirr shop**.

The standard loyalty card gives:

* **1 point for every 10 ETB**

The holiday loyalty card gives:

* **2 points for every 10 ETB**

Customers can also redeem their points, and the balance cannot go below zero.

Each loyalty card keeps its own private and independent points balance.
