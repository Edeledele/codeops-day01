Hello, Ethiopia!

Welcome to my Ethiopian land People: Ethiopia has 80+ ethnic groups (Oromo, Amhara, Tigray, Somali, and more),
each with own bold language and customs.
Amharic is the main official language.
Population is mostly Ethiopian Orthodox Christian and Muslim, living side by side for centuries.

Food: Food: Built around injera — a spongy sourdough
flatbread — eaten with wat (stews) made from lentils, meat, or veggies spiced with berbere. Eaten communally from one big plate, often by hand.
Gursha (feeding someone else a bite) is a sign of love/respect.

Ethiopian Products

  • Coffee
  • Teff (injera flour)
  • Handwoven cotton scarves (habesha kemis fabric)
